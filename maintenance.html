Transparent AMC Contracts

No hidden charges, no surprises. Our Annual Maintenance Contracts include:

  • Fixed monthly pricing with clearly defined scope

Structured Maintenance Visits

Every visit follows our proven 8-step maintenance protocol:

  • Step 1: Visual health inspection of all plants
  • Step 2: Soil moisture and drainage check
  • Step 3: Watering and fertilization as needed
  • Step 4: Pruning dead/diseased foliage
  • Step 5: Pest and disease treatment
  • Step 6: Pot and surface cleaning
  • Step 7: Plant rotation for even growth
  • Step 8: Documentation and client reporting

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our maintenance services

How often will you visit for maintenance?

Visit frequency depends on your chosen plan: Economy (monthly), Basic (bi-weekly), Premium (weekly), and Corporate Elite (2x per week + on-call). We can also customize schedules to your specific needs.

What happens if a plant dies?

Plant replacement policies vary by plan. Premium and Corporate Elite plans include unlimited replacements (when justified by natural causes). Basic plans include 1 replacement per quarter. Economy plans charge separately for replacements. Plants on Rent includes free unlimited replacements.

Do I need to be present during maintenance visits?

No, you don't need to be present. We coordinate with your facilities team or reception to access the premises. We provide detailed visit reports and photos via email after each service.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade anytime with immediate effect. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal period. We'll pro-rate any changes to ensure fair pricing.

What products do you use for pest control?

We use only eco-friendly, non-toxic pest management solutions that are safe for office environments. Our integrated pest management (IPM) approach prioritizes prevention and uses chemical treatments only when absolutely necessary.

Is there a minimum contract period?

Most AMC plans require a 12-month commitment, while Corporate Elite requires 24 months. Plants on Rent has a flexible 3-month minimum. This ensures we can deliver consistent results and maintain plant health.
