Our Chennai Services

Tech Office Plant Solutions

Specialized plant selection for air-conditioned tech offices, startup spaces, and open-plan layouts common in Chennai's IT sector. Low-maintenance varieties perfect for 24/7 office environments.

Campus Landscaping

Comprehensive landscape design for large IT campuses and corporate headquarters. Experience with Infosys, Wipro-style campus greening including outdoor spaces, walkways, and recreational areas.

Vertical Gardens for Tech Parks

Green wall installations designed for Chennai's moderate climate. Perfect for tech park atriums, lobby areas, and exterior facades with automated irrigation systems.
