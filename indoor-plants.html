Natural vs. Artificial Plants

We offer both options - choose what works best for your space

Natural Living Plants

Best for: Well-lit areas, collaborative zones, reception areas, cafeterias

  • Genuine air purification benefits
  • Improved humidity levels
  • Real connection to nature (biophilia)
  • Positive psychological impact on employees
  • Reduced stress and increased productivity
  • Seasonal variety and natural growth

Requires regular maintenance, appropriate lighting, and proper watering schedules.

Premium Artificial Plants

Best for: Low-light areas, conference rooms, basements, zero-maintenance zones

  • Zero maintenance requirements
  • Perfect for low-light or windowless spaces
  • No watering, fertilizing, or care needed
  • Ultra-realistic appearance (silk & synthetic)
  • Permanent aesthetics - always perfect
  • Ideal for high-traffic or event spaces

Premium quality artificial plants that look incredibly realistic and require only occasional dusting.
