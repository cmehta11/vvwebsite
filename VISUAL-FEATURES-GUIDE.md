- **Cards**: Lift up (-8px) with enhanced shadow
- **Buttons**: Color inversion + slight lift
- **Images**: Subtle zoom (1.05x scale)
- **Links**: Underline with color transition

### **Scroll Animations**:
- **Fade-in**: Elements appear as you scroll
- **Staggered**: Items appear one after another
- **Progress Bars**: Fill up when visible
- **Counters**: Numbers count up (1000+, 98%, etc.)

- **Map Markers**: Show city info card
- **Filter Checkboxes**: Update products instantly
- **Sort Dropdown**: Reorder products smoothly
