Quick Answer: Basic Plant Care Formula

Watering: Most indoor plants need water every 7-14 days. Check soil 2" deep - water only when dry.
Light: Bright indirect light works for 80% of office plants. 6-8 hours of filtered light is ideal.
Fertilizer: Feed monthly during growing season (March-October), skip in winter.
