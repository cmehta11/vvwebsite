Quick Answer: What office plants are safe for pets in India?

Top pet-safe office plants: 1) Spider Plant - completely non-toxic; 2) Boston Fern - safe for dogs and cats; 3) Areca Palm - pet-friendly; 4) Parlor Palm - non-toxic; 5) Prayer Plant - safe; 6) Money Tree (Pachira) - pet-safe. AVOID: Peace Lily, Pothos, Philodendron, Snake Plant, ZZ Plant - all toxic to pets.

Source: ASPCA Toxic Plant Database

15 Pet-Friendly Office Plants Safe for Dogs & Cats in India

The complete guide to creating a green, pet-safe workplace for offices with furry visitors

December 5, 2025 8 min read Plant Care, Workplace Wellness

With pet-friendly workplace policies becoming increasingly popular in India's progressive companies—from startups in Bangalore to tech giants in Hyderabad—the question of plant safety has never been more important. Many common office plants can be toxic to dogs and cats, causing symptoms ranging from mild irritation to serious illness.

At Vertvista, we've helped numerous pet-friendly offices create beautiful green spaces without compromising animal safety. This guide covers the 15 best pet-safe plants that thrive in Indian office environments.

Plants to Avoid

Toxic to pets: Pothos, Philodendron, Peace Lily, Dieffenbachia, Aloe Vera, ZZ Plant, Snake Plant (mild toxicity), English Ivy, and most Lilies. If your office has pets, these popular plants should be avoided or placed well out of reach.

Top 15 Pet-Safe Office Plants for India

1

Areca Palm (Dypsis lutescens)

The most popular pet-safe plant for Indian offices. Its feathery fronds add tropical elegance while being completely non-toxic to cats and dogs.

Bright indirect light Weekly watering 100% Pet Safe
2

Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)

Extremely hardy and pet-safe, spider plants produce cascading "babies" that add visual interest. Cats may nibble on them (non-toxic), but excessive eating can cause mild stomach upset.

Low to bright light When soil dry 100% Pet Safe
3

Boston Fern (Nephrolepis exaltata)

Lush, feathery fronds make this a stunning addition to reception areas. Completely safe for pets and excellent for improving air quality and humidity.

Indirect light Keep moist 100% Pet Safe
4

Parlor Palm (Chamaedorea elegans)

A compact palm perfect for desks and small spaces. Tolerates low light conditions common in interior offices. Completely non-toxic.

Low light tolerant Moderate watering 100% Pet Safe

Best Practices for Pet-Friendly Offices

Elevate Plants When Possible

Place plants on high shelves, hanging planters, or tall plant stands to keep them out of reach of curious pets.

Use Pet-Safe Fertilizers

Ensure all fertilizers and pesticides used are non-toxic. Vertvista uses only pet-safe, organic maintenance products.

Cover Soil with Pebbles

Decorative pebbles prevent pets from digging in soil, which can contain harmful fertilizer residue.
