Green Wall Systems

Transform Vertical Spaces into Living Gardens | Natural & Artificial Vertical Gardens for Indoor and Outdoor Applications

Green walls, also known as vertical gardens or living walls, bring nature into architectural spaces by transforming blank walls into vibrant, lush ecosystems.

At Vertvista, we design and install cutting-edge green wall systems that combine horticultural expertise with engineering innovation. Whether you need indoor air purification, outdoor façade beautification, or acoustic improvement, our vertical gardens deliver measurable benefits while creating stunning visual impact.

With over 12 years of experience and 1000+ installations across India, we've perfected the art and science of vertical greening for corporate offices, commercial buildings, hospitality spaces, residential complexes, and public institutions.

Our Green Wall Systems

Tailored solutions for every space and requirement

Natural Living Walls

Real plants growing in engineered systems with integrated irrigation and drainage. Perfect for spaces that prioritize air quality, biophilic design, and authentic natural beauty.

  • Hydroponic or soil-based growing systems
  • Automated drip irrigation with timers
  • Active air purification (removes VOCs, CO₂)
  • Lightweight engineered panels (20-40 kg/sqm)
  • Continuous plant health monitoring
  • Monthly AMC available
Best for: Office lobbies, atriums, indoor spaces, biophilic design projects

Artificial Green Walls

Premium UV-resistant artificial foliage panels that require zero maintenance while delivering the visual impact of natural greenery. Ideal for high-traffic or challenging environments.

  • Zero water or irrigation requirements
  • No maintenance contracts needed
  • UV-resistant, fade-proof materials
  • Fire-rated options available
  • Extremely lightweight (<10 kg/sqm)
  • 5-7 year lifespan with color guarantee
Best for: Outdoor facades, malls, airports, restaurants, low-light areas

Hybrid Systems

Strategic combination of natural and artificial elements, delivering the best of both worlds. Natural plants in key viewing areas, artificial in challenging zones.

  • Optimized cost-benefit ratio
  • Natural plants at eye level for maximum impact
  • Artificial in high/unreachable areas
  • Seamless visual integration
  • Reduced maintenance requirements
  • Budget-friendly alternative
Best for: Large installations, budget-conscious projects, challenging environments

Why Choose Green Walls?

Science-backed benefits for your space

Air Quality Enhancement

Natural green walls actively filter airborne pollutants, absorb CO₂, and release oxygen, improving indoor air quality by up to 60%.

60% cleaner air

Temperature Regulation

Vertical gardens provide natural insulation, reducing wall surface temperatures by 5-10°C and lowering cooling costs by up to 30%.

30% energy savings

Acoustic Improvement

Dense foliage absorbs sound waves, reducing ambient noise by 15-40 dB, creating quieter, more productive environments.

40 dB noise reduction

Psychological Wellbeing

Biophilic elements reduce stress by 37%, increase productivity by 15%, and improve focus and creativity in work environments.

37% stress reduction

Aesthetic Excellence

Transform bland walls into living artworks. Green walls become focal points that enhance property value and brand image.

Premium appeal

Biodiversity Support

Outdoor green walls create micro-habitats for birds, butterflies, and beneficial insects, contributing to urban biodiversity.

Eco-friendly

Humidity Balance

Natural systems release moisture through transpiration, maintaining optimal 40-60% humidity levels for comfort and health.

Optimal humidity

Green Building Credits

Earn LEED, IGBC, and GRIHA points for your project. Green walls contribute to multiple sustainability certification criteria.

Certification ready

Our Installation Process

From concept to completion

1

Site Assessment & Consultation

Our team visits your site to assess wall conditions, light availability, water access, and structural capacity. We discuss your aesthetic preferences, budget, and maintenance expectations.

2

Custom Design & Plant Selection

Based on site conditions, we create 3D design renderings and select appropriate plant species. Natural, artificial, or hybrid system is recommended with detailed specifications.

3

Structural Preparation

Wall preparation includes waterproofing membrane, mounting frame installation, and integration of irrigation and drainage systems. Electrical connections completed for automated systems.

4

Panel Installation & Planting

Pre-grown panels or modular systems are installed on mounting frames. For natural systems, plants are carefully positioned and secured. Irrigation system tested thoroughly.

5

Initial Establishment & Training

First 3-6 months of intensive care ensures proper plant establishment. We provide staff training on basic monitoring and share emergency contact protocols.

6

Ongoing Maintenance & Support

Transition to regular AMC schedule with monthly visits. Continuous monitoring, seasonal plant rotation, pest management, and system optimization ensure long-term success.
