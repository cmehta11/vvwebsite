Biophilic Design Glossary

60+ essential terms and definitions for indoor plants, green walls, and workplace biophilia

60+ Terms 8 Categories Updated Dec 2025

A

Terms starting with A

Acclimatization

The process of gradually adjusting plants to new environmental conditions (light, temperature, humidity) before final placement. Typically takes 2-4 weeks for indoor plants moving from nursery to office conditions.

Plant Care Installation

AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract)

A service agreement for regular plant maintenance including watering, pruning, fertilization, pest control, and plant replacement. AMCs in India typically range from ₹150-300 per plant per month, depending on plant size and service frequency.

Services Maintenance
Related: Maintenance Services

Air-Purifying Plants

Plants that actively remove toxins like formaldehyde, benzene, and xylene from indoor air. NASA research identified top performers including Snake Plant, Peace Lily, and Spider Plant. One plant per 100 sq. ft. is recommended for effective air purification.

Plant Types Health

Areca Palm (Dypsis lutescens)

A popular indoor palm known for its feathery fronds and air-purifying properties. Acts as a natural humidifier, making it ideal for air-conditioned offices. Grows 1.5-2m tall indoors and requires medium indirect light.

Plant Species Air Purifying
B

Terms starting with B

Biophilia

The innate human tendency to seek connections with nature and other living systems. The term was popularized by biologist E.O. Wilson in 1984. This biological affinity for nature forms the scientific basis for biophilic design.

Core Concept Science

Biophilic Design

An architectural and interior design approach that incorporates natural elements, patterns, materials, and views of nature into the built environment. Research shows it can increase productivity by 15%, reduce stress by 37%, and improve creativity by 15%.

Design Core Concept
Related: Our Biophilic Design Services

Biowall

Another term for a living wall or green wall, particularly one designed with integrated irrigation and growing systems. Biowalls are often modular, making them easier to install and maintain in corporate environments.

Green Walls Installation
C

Terms starting with C

Circadian Lighting

Lighting systems designed to mimic the natural progression of daylight, supporting human circadian rhythms. A key biophilic design element that can improve sleep quality, mood, and productivity when combined with plants.

Biophilic Design Wellness

Cortisol

The primary stress hormone in humans. Studies show that exposure to plants and natural elements can reduce cortisol levels by 13-16%, explaining the calming effect of biophilic environments.

Health Science
D

Terms starting with D

Drip Irrigation

An automated watering system that delivers water directly to plant roots through a network of tubes and emitters. Essential for green walls and large plant installations, reducing water usage by 30-60% compared to manual watering.

Irrigation Technology

Dracaena

A genus of over 120 species of trees and succulent shrubs popular for indoor use. Varieties like Dracaena marginata and Dracaena fragrans are excellent low-light plants that remove benzene and formaldehyde from air.

Plant Species Low Light
F

Terms starting with F

FRP Planters

Fibre-Reinforced Plastic planters that are lightweight, durable, and weather-resistant. Vertvista manufactures custom FRP planters in-house, offering unlimited design possibilities for corporate spaces.

Planters Materials
Related: Our Planters Collection

Fertigation

The application of fertilizers through an irrigation system. Commonly used in green wall maintenance to deliver balanced nutrients directly to plant roots, ensuring consistent growth and health.

Maintenance Technology
G

Terms starting with G

Green Wall

A vertical structure covered with living plants grown in modular panels or containers. Also called living walls, vertical gardens, or biowalls. Costs in India range from ₹2,000-4,500 per sq. ft. depending on system type and plant selection.

Core Concept Installation
Related: Green Wall Systems

Green Façade

An exterior wall covered with climbing plants or vines supported by cables or trellises. Different from green walls, which use modular growing systems. Vertvista uses Jakob cable systems for premium green façades.

Exterior Architecture
Related: Green Façade Systems
I

Terms starting with I

Indoor Air Quality (IAQ)

The air quality within buildings, particularly concerning occupant health. Indoor air can be 2-5x more polluted than outdoor air. Plants improve IAQ by absorbing VOCs, producing oxygen, and increasing humidity.

Health Science

IGBC (Indian Green Building Council)

India's leading green building certification body, part of CII. IGBC certifications include points for biophilic design elements, indoor plants, and living walls as part of Indoor Environmental Quality criteria.

Certification India
L

Terms starting with L

LEED (Leadership in Energy & Environmental Design)

The most widely used green building rating system globally. Biophilic design elements including indoor plants and green walls can contribute 5-10 points toward LEED certification, primarily in Indoor Environmental Quality category.

Certification International

Living Wall

A wall covered with living plants grown in a structural support system, with integrated irrigation and drainage. Synonymous with green wall, biowall, and vertical garden.

Green Walls Design

Low-Light Plants

Plants that thrive in indirect light or fluorescent lighting conditions typical of offices. Examples include Snake Plant, ZZ Plant, Pothos, and Aglaonema. Essential for interior spaces away from windows.

Plant Types Office
M

Terms starting with M

Moss Art / Moss Wall

Preserved moss (typically reindeer or sheet moss from Scandinavia) used to create artistic wall installations. Requires zero maintenance, no watering, and no sunlight. Costs range from ₹3,500-6,000 per sq. ft. in India.

Design Zero Maintenance
Related: Moss Art Solutions

Modular System

A green wall system using pre-planted panels or modules that connect together. Allows for easy installation, plant replacement, and design flexibility. The most common system type for corporate installations.

Green Walls Technology
N

Terms starting with N

NASA Clean Air Study

A landmark 1989 NASA research project that identified indoor plants effective at removing air pollutants. The study tested 12 plant species and found they could remove up to 87% of air toxins in 24 hours.

Research Air Quality

Nature Deficit Disorder

A term coined by Richard Louv describing the human costs of alienation from nature, including diminished use of senses, attention difficulties, and higher rates of physical and emotional illness. Biophilic design addresses this in built environments.

Health Concept
P

Terms starting with P

Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

Also called Money Plant or Devil's Ivy, one of the most popular indoor plants globally. Extremely hardy, tolerates low light and neglect, excellent air purifier, and grows as a trailing vine ideal for shelves and green walls.

Plant Species Easy Care

Preserved Plants

Real plants that have been treated with glycerin-based preservatives to maintain their appearance without any maintenance. They look and feel natural but require no water, light, or care. Lifespan of 5-10 years.

Plant Types Zero Maintenance

Phytoremediation

The use of plants to remove, degrade, or contain environmental pollutants. In indoor contexts, this refers to plants absorbing VOCs and other air pollutants through their leaves and roots.

Science Air Quality
S

Terms starting with S

Snake Plant (Sansevieria trifasciata)

Also known as Mother-in-Law's Tongue, one of the hardiest indoor plants. Tolerates extreme low light, infrequent watering, and releases oxygen at night. NASA-certified air purifier, ideal for bedrooms and offices.

Plant Species Very Easy

Sick Building Syndrome (SBS)

A condition where building occupants experience acute health effects linked to time spent in a building, with no specific illness identified. Symptoms include headaches, fatigue, and respiratory issues. Plants can reduce SBS by improving air quality.

Health Buildings
V

Terms starting with V

Vertical Garden

A garden grown on a vertically suspended panel using hydroponics or soil-based systems. Can be free-standing or attached to walls. Synonymous with green wall and living wall.

Green Walls Design

VOC (Volatile Organic Compound)

Organic chemicals that easily become vapors or gases, often released from paints, furniture, and cleaning products. Common indoor VOCs include formaldehyde, benzene, and toluene. Plants can absorb and break down many VOCs.

Air Quality Health
W

Terms starting with W

WELL Building Standard

A performance-based certification system measuring building features that impact human health and wellbeing. Includes specific credits for biophilic design, indoor plants, and nature views. Growing rapidly in India's corporate sector.

Certification Wellness

Workplace Biophilia

The application of biophilic design principles specifically in office and work environments. Focuses on improving employee wellbeing, productivity, creativity, and job satisfaction through nature integration.

Corporate Wellness
Z

Terms starting with Z

ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

One of the most indestructible indoor plants, tolerating extreme low light, drought, and neglect. Glossy leaves add a modern aesthetic. Only needs watering every 3-4 weeks. Perfect for offices with minimal natural light.

Plant Species Very Easy
