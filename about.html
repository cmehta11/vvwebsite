About Vertvista Eco Solutions

India's Trusted Biophilia & Greening Partner

We create healthier, more vibrant, and productive environments through expert biophilic design and landscape solutions.

Vertvista Eco Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is India's leading provider of comprehensive biophilia and landscaping services. For over 12 years, we've been transforming spaces across the country—from corporate offices and commercial buildings to residential complexes and public institutions.

We believe that people thrive when surrounded by nature. Our mission is to make cities healthier and more inspiring by integrating greenery with innovative design and reliable operations.

Our Story

Building India's most trusted biophilia & greening partner

Founded with Vision

Established with a mission to bring nature back into India's rapidly urbanizing spaces, we started by serving corporate offices in Mumbai.

Steady Growth

Over 12+ years, we've expanded our presence across 6 major cities and completed 1000+ projects, earning the trust of 200+ clients.

Industry Leadership
