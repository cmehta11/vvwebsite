Office Plant Rental vs Purchase: Which Option is Best for Your Business?

December 5, 2025 12 min read

One of the most common questions we get from corporate clients in India is: "Should we rent or buy our office plants?" It's a valid question with significant financial implications, especially for large office spaces with hundreds of plants.

The answer isn't always straightforward - it depends on your budget, timeline, maintenance capabilities, and business goals. This guide will help you make an informed decision based on real data from our 12+ years of serving Indian corporate clients.

Quick Comparison: Rental vs Purchase

Factor Rental Purchase
Upfront Cost Low (first month only) High (full plant cost)
Monthly Cost ₹8,500 - ₹75,000+ ₹0 (or AMC: ₹3,000-15,000)
Maintenance Included (100%) Your responsibility (or AMC)
Replacement Free - included Additional cost
Flexibility High - easy changes Low - stuck with what you buy
Ownership Never own the plants Full ownership
Break-even Point N/A 18-24 months typically

Understanding Plant Rental in India

Plant rental (also called plant leasing) is a service where you pay a monthly fee for plants plus complete maintenance. The rental company owns the plants and is responsible for keeping them healthy.

What's Included in Plant Rental?

  • Plants: High-quality, acclimatized plants suited to your space
  • Planters: Premium decorative containers (usually included)
  • Delivery & Installation: Professional setup
  • Weekly/Bi-weekly Maintenance: Watering, pruning, fertilizing
  • Free Replacements: Dying or damaged plants swapped at no cost
  • Seasonal Rotation: Option to refresh plants periodically
  • Pest Control: Treatment if issues arise

Typical Rental Costs in India

Package Plants Included Monthly Cost
Starter 20-30 small-medium plants ₹8,500 - ₹12,000
Professional 50-70 mixed plants ₹15,000 - ₹25,000
Enterprise 100-150 plants + statement pieces ₹30,000 - ₹50,000
Campus 200+ plants, multiple floors ₹50,000 - ₹1,00,000+

Rental Pros

  • Zero upfront investment
  • All maintenance included
  • Free plant replacements
  • Flexibility to scale up/down
  • No risk of plant death
  • Professional expertise
  • Predictable monthly costs

Rental Cons

  • Never own the assets
  • Higher long-term cost (3+ years)
  • Minimum contract periods
  • Less control over plant choice
  • Dependent on vendor quality

Understanding Plant Purchase

When you purchase plants, you own them outright. You're responsible for their care, though many companies opt for an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) to handle upkeep.

Typical Purchase Costs

Plant Type Price Range
Small desk plants (4-6 inch) ₹150 - ₹500
Medium plants (8-12 inch) ₹500 - ₹1,500
Large floor plants (2-4 ft) ₹1,500 - ₹5,000
Statement plants (5-8 ft) ₹5,000 - ₹15,000
Premium planters ₹500 - ₹5,000 each

AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) Costs

If you purchase plants, we strongly recommend an AMC:

  • Basic AMC: ₹3,000 - ₹7,000/month (up to 50 plants)
  • Standard AMC: ₹7,000 - ₹15,000/month (50-100 plants)
  • Premium AMC: ₹15,000+/month (100+ plants, includes replacements)

Purchase Pros

  • Own the assets
  • Lower long-term cost (3+ years)
  • Full control over selection
  • No monthly rental fees
  • One-time tax deduction
  • Freedom to choose any vendor for AMC

Purchase Cons

  • High upfront investment
  • Risk of plant death
  • Replacement costs extra
  • Requires in-house expertise or AMC
  • Depreciation of assets
  • Less flexibility

Which Option is Right for You?

Choose Rental If:

  • You're a startup or small business with limited upfront capital
  • You're in a leased/rented office space
  • You have no in-house facilities team
  • You want zero hassle and complete peace of mind
  • You might relocate or expand in the next 2 years
  • You prefer OpEx over CapEx

Choose Purchase If:

  • You own your office space or have a long-term lease (5+ years)
  • You have budget for upfront investment
  • You have a facilities team for day-to-day oversight
  • You want long-term cost savings
  • You prefer CapEx for tax benefits
  • You want full control over plant selection

The Hybrid Approach: Best of Both Worlds

Many of our clients opt for a hybrid model:

  • Purchase: Large statement plants for permanent areas (lobby, boardroom)
  • Rent: Desk plants and flexible areas (workstations, temporary spaces)

This gives you ownership of high-impact assets while maintaining flexibility for the rest.

Conclusion

Both rental and purchase have their merits. Rental is ideal for businesses that want hassle-free greenery with predictable costs, while purchase makes sense for companies with long-term space commitments and available capital.

Regardless of which option you choose, the key is working with a reputable provider who understands Indian office conditions and can deliver quality plants with reliable service.
