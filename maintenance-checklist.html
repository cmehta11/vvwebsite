Plant Maintenance Checklist

Free interactive tool to track and manage your indoor plant, green wall, and landscape maintenance tasks

Quick Answer: Plant Maintenance Schedule

Daily: Visual inspection for pests/stress.
Weekly: Water when top 2-3" soil is dry, check drainage.
Bi-Weekly: Dust leaves, rotate plants for even growth.
Monthly: Fertilize (growing season), prune dead leaves.
Quarterly: Deep inspection, check roots, clean planters.
Annually: Repot if needed, replace struggling plants.

Tools You'll Need

Watering can
Pruning shears
Soft cloth
Liquid fertilizer
Moisture meter

Seasonal Care Tips for India

Summer (March-May)

  • Increase watering frequency
  • Move plants from direct afternoon sun
  • Use mulch to retain moisture
  • Mist plants in dry weather

Monsoon (June-Sept)

  • Reduce watering drastically
  • Improve drainage
  • Watch for fungal diseases
  • Apply neem oil for pests

Autumn (Oct-Nov)

  • Resume normal watering
  • Perfect time for repotting
  • Apply slow-release fertilizer
  • Prune and shape plants

Winter (Dec-Feb)

  • Reduce watering frequency
  • Stop fertilizing dormant plants
  • Move tropical plants indoors
  • Protect from cold drafts
