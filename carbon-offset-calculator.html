How Much CO₂ Do Office Plants Absorb?

Plants are natural carbon capture systems: A single large indoor plant absorbs 35-50 kg CO₂ annually. A 100 sq ft living green wall absorbs ~230 kg CO₂/year - equivalent to driving 575 km in a car. Indoor plants also remove up to 87% of air toxins (NASA study) and increase oxygen levels by 10-25%.
