Green Façade System Types

Most Popular

Cable & Wire Rope Systems

High-tensile stainless steel cables create a minimal, modern framework for climbing plants. Ideal for contemporary architecture where the structure should remain subtle.

  • Sleek, nearly invisible support structure
  • Suitable for heights up to 50+ meters
  • Works with fast-growing climbers
  • Minimal building penetration required
  • Custom spacing configurations
Heavy-Duty

Modular Panel Systems

Prefabricated steel or aluminum grid panels offer robust support for denser vegetation coverage. Perfect for projects requiring faster green coverage.

  • Faster plant coverage development
  • Standardized modules for efficiency
  • Supports heavier plant loads
  • Easy maintenance access
  • Powder-coated color options
Maximum Coverage

Mesh & Net Systems

Fine stainless steel mesh provides comprehensive coverage support, allowing plants to fill every available space. Best for achieving a complete green wall effect.

  • Complete surface coverage potential
  • Suitable for delicate climbing plants
  • Creates uniform green appearance
  • Excellent for wind-exposed areas
  • Various mesh sizes available

Our Turnkey Installation Process

From initial consultation to ongoing care, Vertvista handles every detail

1

Site Assessment & Design

Our experts conduct a comprehensive site survey, analyzing building structure, sun exposure, wind patterns, and drainage requirements. We develop a customized design with Jakob system specifications and plant selections perfectly suited to your environment.

2

Structural Engineering

Load calculations, mounting point analysis, and structural integration planning. We coordinate with building engineers to ensure safe, code-compliant installations with proper anchoring and support.

3

System Installation

Professional installation of Jakob cable systems, planter integration, irrigation setup, and drainage connections. All work performed by certified technicians following manufacturer specifications.

4

Planting & Establishment

Strategic plant placement in custom Vertvista planters, initial training on cable systems, soil preparation, and initial watering system testing. Growth monitoring during establishment period.

5

Ongoing Maintenance

Scheduled pruning and training, irrigation system monitoring, plant health assessments, seasonal fertilization, and replacement of any underperforming plants. Optional AMC contracts available.
