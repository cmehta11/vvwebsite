General Questions

Biophilic design is the practice of connecting people with nature within built environments. It matters because studies show that incorporating natural elements like plants, natural light, and organic materials can increase productivity by 15%, reduce stress by 37%, and improve air quality by up to 30%.

At Vertvista, we specialize in creating these connections through indoor plants, green walls, moss art, and complete landscape solutions tailored for corporate workspaces.

Technical Questions

For offices with limited natural light, we recommend hardy, low-light tolerant species:

  • Snake Plant (Sansevieria): Extremely tolerant, air-purifying
  • Pothos: Trailing vines, very adaptable
  • ZZ Plant: Drought-tolerant, glossy foliage
  • Peace Lily: Beautiful white flowers, air-purifying
  • Dracaena varieties: Architectural form, low maintenance
  • Aglaonema (Chinese Evergreen): Colorful foliage, tolerates neglect

We conduct a lighting assessment during our site visit to recommend the best species mix for your specific conditions.

Our green wall systems feature sophisticated irrigation and drainage:

  • Automated drip irrigation: Programmable timers deliver precise water amounts
  • Recirculation systems: Water is collected at the bottom and recirculated, reducing waste by 60%
  • Drainage layers: Proprietary mat system prevents waterlogging and root rot
  • Water quality management: Filtration and fertilizer injection systems
  • Remote monitoring: Optional smart sensors track moisture levels

Installation includes waterproofing, drainage connection to existing plumbing, and electrical hookup for pumps.

Green wall weight varies by system type:

  • Lightweight modular systems: 30-50 kg/sq.m (suitable for most modern walls)
  • Soil-based systems: 80-120 kg/sq.m (requires structural assessment)
  • Hydroponic systems: 20-40 kg/sq.m (lightest option)

We always conduct structural assessments before installation and can engineer solutions for weight-sensitive walls, including freestanding frames if needed.

Sustainability & Environment

Sustainability is core to our mission:

  • Water conservation: Drip irrigation reduces water use by 60%, green wall recirculation systems
  • Organic fertilizers: Compost-based nutrition, minimal chemical inputs
  • Local sourcing: 80% of plants from Indian nurseries, supporting local economy
  • Recycled materials: FRP planters can be made from recycled plastics
  • Zero-waste goal: Plant waste composted, pots reused or recycled

We maintain a network of certified nurseries:

  • Indoor plants: 80% from Indian nurseries (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra)
  • Specialty species: 15% imported from Thailand, Netherlands (with proper phytosanitary certificates)
  • Moss: Preserved moss ethically harvested from Europe and Georgia
  • Native plants: Priority for outdoor landscapes to support local biodiversity

All suppliers audited for ethical practices and plant health standards.

We follow integrated pest management (IPM) principles:

  • Prevention first: Proper plant selection, spacing, and care reduce pest issues by 80%
  • Natural solutions: Neem oil, horticultural soaps, beneficial insects
  • Chemical pesticides: Only as absolute last resort (<5% of cases)
  • When chemicals necessary: Lowest toxicity options, after-hours application, 48-hour notice

All products used are safe for office environments and comply with green building certifications.
