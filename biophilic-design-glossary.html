Quick Definition: What is Biophilic Design?

Biophilic design is an evidence-based architectural approach that incorporates natural elements—plants, natural light, water, natural materials, and organic patterns—into built environments to satisfy humans' innate need to connect with nature (biophilia). Studies show it increases productivity by 8-15%, reduces stress by 37%, improves air quality, and boosts employee satisfaction by 15%. In India, leading companies like Microsoft, Google, Wipro, and Morgan Stanley have adopted biophilic design in their offices.

Source: Human Spaces Report 2015, World Green Building Council

Plants

Air Purifying Plants

Plants that actively remove toxins, VOCs (Volatile Organic Compounds), and pollutants from indoor air through their leaves and roots. NASA's Clean Air Study identified top performers including Snake Plant (Sansevieria), Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum), Pothos, Spider Plant, and Areca Palm. In Indian offices, these plants help combat pollution from AC systems, new furniture, and outdoor air quality issues.

NASA Study VOCs Indoor Air Quality
Plants

Areca Palm (सुपारी पाम)

Dypsis lutescens, also known as Golden Cane Palm or Butterfly Palm. One of India's most popular office plants, known for its air-purifying abilities, natural humidification, and elegant appearance. Releases approximately 1 liter of water per day, making it ideal for AC environments. Grows well in indirect light and reaches 6-7 feet indoors.

Air Purifying Natural Humidifier Low Maintenance
Wellness

Attention Restoration Theory (ART)

A psychological theory developed by Rachel and Stephen Kaplan proposing that exposure to natural environments helps restore depleted attention and mental fatigue. The theory explains why office workers exposed to plants and natural views show improved concentration, reduced mental fatigue, and better cognitive performance.

Cognitive Performance Mental Restoration Nature Views

Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)

In Indian commercial landscaping, an AMC is a service agreement for ongoing plant care and maintenance. Typically includes weekly/bi-weekly visits, watering, pruning, fertilizing, pest control, and plant replacement guarantees. Vertvista AMC plans start at ₹8,500/month and include 100% replacement guarantee for any plant that doesn't thrive.

B

Biophilic Design

Biophilia

The innate human tendency to seek connections with nature and other forms of life. Coined by psychologist Erich Fromm in 1964 and popularized by biologist Edward O. Wilson in his 1984 book "Biophilia." Wilson proposed that humans have an evolutionary predisposition to affiliate with natural systems, which explains our psychological and physiological responses to natural environments.

Edward O. Wilson Human-Nature Connection Evolutionary Psychology
Biophilic Design

Biophilic Design

An evidence-based design philosophy that incorporates natural elements into built environments to satisfy humans' biophilic needs. The concept was formalized by Stephen Kellert and includes 14 patterns across three categories: Nature in the Space, Natural Analogues, and Nature of the Space. Research shows biophilic workplaces see 8-15% productivity increases, 37% stress reduction, and 15% higher creativity.

14 Patterns Terrapin Bright Green Productivity
Biophilic Design

Biomorphic Forms

Design elements that mimic natural shapes, patterns, and textures found in nature. Examples include leaf-shaped furniture, wave-patterned carpets, organic curved walls, and fractal-based artwork. Biomorphic forms trigger positive psychological responses even when actual living nature isn't present.

Natural Patterns Organic Shapes Fractals
Technical

Building-Integrated Vegetation (BIV)

The integration of plants and green systems directly into building architecture, including green roofs, living walls, green facades, and planted balconies. BIV systems provide thermal insulation, reduce urban heat island effect, improve air quality, and enhance building aesthetics.

Green Roofs Living Walls Sustainable Architecture

C

Wellness

Circadian Lighting

Lighting systems designed to mimic natural daylight patterns throughout the day, supporting human circadian rhythms. In biophilic design, circadian lighting complements natural light and plant arrangements to create healthier work environments. Typically uses warm light (2700K) in mornings and evenings, cooler light (5000K+) during midday.

Dynamic Lighting Natural Light Wellness
Plants

Chlorophytum (Spider Plant)

One of the easiest air-purifying plants for Indian offices. Removes formaldehyde, xylene, and toluene. Produces "babies" (plantlets) that can be propagated. Tolerates inconsistent watering and low light. NASA rates it among the top 3 plants for air purification.

Air Purifying Beginner-Friendly Propagation
India-Specific

Corporate Wellness Programs (India)

Workplace health initiatives increasingly common in Indian IT parks and corporate offices. Biophilic design is a key component, with companies like Infosys, TCS, Wipro, and Google India incorporating extensive plantscaping. Studies show ROI of ₹3-5 return for every ₹1 invested in workplace wellness through reduced absenteeism and improved productivity.

Employee Wellness IT Parks Productivity ROI

D

Technical

Drip Irrigation

A water-efficient irrigation method delivering water directly to plant roots through a network of tubes and emitters. Essential for green wall and large indoor plantscape maintenance. Reduces water waste by 30-50% compared to traditional watering and allows precise nutrient delivery through fertigation.

Water Efficiency Fertigation Green Wall Systems
Biophilic Design

Daylighting

The practice of using natural light to illuminate building interiors through windows, skylights, and light wells. A core biophilic design element that reduces energy costs, improves occupant well-being, and supports plant growth in interior landscapes. Indian green building standards (IGBC, GRIHA) prioritize daylighting.

Natural Light IGBC Energy Efficiency

E

Technical

Evapotranspiration

The combined process of water evaporation from soil and plant surfaces and transpiration through plant leaves. Indoor plants increase humidity through evapotranspiration, which helps counteract dry air from air conditioning—particularly beneficial in Indian offices during summer and in dry climates like Rajasthan and Gujarat.

Humidity Natural Cooling AC Environments
Plants

Epipremnum aureum (Money Plant/Pothos)

India's most popular indoor plant, known locally as Money Plant (मनी प्लांट). Extremely hardy, tolerates low light and irregular watering, removes formaldehyde and benzene from air. In Indian culture, associated with prosperity and good luck. Perfect for office beginners and can trail or climb.

Low Maintenance Air Purifying Trailing Plant

F

Biophilic Design

Fractal Patterns

Self-repeating patterns at different scales found throughout nature (fern fronds, tree branches, river networks). Research shows viewing fractal patterns reduces stress by up to 60%. Biophilic design incorporates fractals through actual plants, nature photography, artwork, and architectural elements.

Natural Patterns Stress Reduction Visual Complexity
Plants

Ficus Species

A diverse genus of plants commonly used in Indian offices including Ficus benjamina (Weeping Fig), Ficus elastica (Rubber Plant), Ficus lyrata (Fiddle Leaf Fig), and Ficus microcarpa. Known for air purification, visual impact, and adaptability to indoor conditions. Rubber Plant is particularly effective at removing formaldehyde.

Statement Plants Air Purifying Indoor Trees
Technical

Fertigation

The practice of applying fertilizers through irrigation systems, particularly drip irrigation. Used in commercial green wall and large-scale plantscape maintenance to deliver precise nutrients to plants. Ensures consistent plant health with minimal labor.

Drip Irrigation Plant Nutrition Commercial Maintenance

G

Green Walls

Green Wall

A vertical surface covered with living plants, either growing in soil, hydroponic media, or felt pockets. Also called living walls, vertical gardens, or plant walls. Green walls provide air purification, noise reduction (8-10 dB), thermal insulation, aesthetic appeal, and biophilic benefits. In India, popular for corporate lobbies, IT parks, and hospitality venues.

Living Wall Vertical Garden Air Purification
Green Walls

Green Façade

A building exterior covered with climbing plants growing on support structures (trellises, cables, mesh). Unlike living walls with planted modules, green façades use plants rooted at ground level that climb upward. Common in Indian cities for building cooling—can reduce wall temperatures by 10-15°C during summer.

Climbing Plants Building Cooling Exterior Greening
Sustainability

Green Building Certification

Third-party verification that a building meets environmental and sustainability standards. In India, key certifications include IGBC (Indian Green Building Council), GRIHA (Green Rating for Integrated Habitat Assessment), and international standards like LEED and WELL. Biophilic design elements earn credits in most systems.

IGBC LEED GRIHA
India-Specific

GRIHA (Green Rating for Integrated Habitat Assessment)

India's national rating system for green buildings, developed by TERI (The Energy and Resources Institute). Evaluates buildings on 34 criteria including site planning, water management, energy efficiency, and indoor environmental quality. Biophilic design elements contribute to multiple GRIHA criteria.

TERI Indian Standards Sustainability

H

Technical

Hydroponics

A method of growing plants without soil, using mineral nutrient solutions in water. Many commercial green wall systems use hydroponic or semi-hydroponic methods. Benefits include lighter weight, cleaner installation, reduced pest issues, and precise nutrient control. Common in Indian commercial plantscaping.

Soilless Growing Green Walls Nutrient Solutions
Wellness

Human-Centered Design

A design approach that prioritizes human needs, behaviors, and well-being. Biophilic design is a subset of human-centered design, recognizing that humans have innate needs for nature connection that should be addressed in built environments. Focuses on comfort, productivity, health, and happiness.

User Experience Wellness Design Occupant Comfort

I

India-Specific

IGBC (Indian Green Building Council)

Part of the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), IGBC is India's leading green building certification body. Offers ratings including IGBC Green Homes, IGBC Green Factory, IGBC Green Office, and IGBC Green Landscape. Biophilic design and plantscaping earn credits under multiple categories including innovation and indoor environmental quality.

CII Green Certification Sustainable Building
Plants

Indoor Plants

Plants adapted to grow in indoor conditions with limited natural light, controlled temperatures, and often air conditioning. For Indian offices, recommended species include Snake Plant, Money Plant, Areca Palm, ZZ Plant, Peace Lily, Rubber Plant, and Dracaena. Selection depends on light levels, maintenance capacity, and aesthetic goals.

Office Plants Houseplants Interior Landscaping
Wellness

Indoor Air Quality (IAQ)

The quality of air inside buildings, affected by pollutants, humidity, temperature, and ventilation. Poor IAQ causes Sick Building Syndrome. Plants improve IAQ by removing VOCs, producing oxygen, and regulating humidity. Particularly important in Indian metros where outdoor air quality is often poor.

Air Purification VOCs Sick Building Syndrome

K

Biophilic Design

Kellert, Stephen

Yale professor and pioneer of biophilic design who developed the theoretical framework connecting biophilia to architecture. His 2008 book "Biophilic Design: The Theory, Science, and Practice of Bringing Buildings to Life" established foundational principles still used today. Identified six elements and 70+ attributes of biophilic design.

Biophilic Design Theory Yale Design Framework

L

Green Walls

Living Wall

A wall system supporting living plants in a vertical orientation. Types include modular panel systems, felt pocket systems, tray systems, and freestanding units. Living walls require irrigation, drainage, and ongoing maintenance but provide superior biophilic benefits compared to preserved alternatives. Costs in India range from ₹1,500-8,000/sq.ft. depending on system and plant selection.

Green Wall Vertical Garden Plant Wall Systems
Sustainability

LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design)

Internationally recognized green building certification developed by USGBC. Many Indian corporate offices pursue LEED certification. Biophilic design elements can contribute to multiple LEED credits including Indoor Environmental Quality, Innovation, and Regional Priority credits.

USGBC Green Building International Certification
Technical

Low-Light Plants

Plants adapted to survive and thrive in conditions below 100 foot-candles (typical office lighting). Essential for Indian offices with limited windows or interior spaces. Top performers include ZZ Plant, Snake Plant, Pothos, Aglaonema, and Dracaena. Can survive on fluorescent or LED lighting alone.

Shade-Tolerant Office Conditions ZZ Plant

M

Green Walls

Moss Art / Moss Wall

Decorative panels made from preserved (stabilized) moss arranged in artistic patterns. Unlike living walls, moss art requires no watering, light, or maintenance—the moss is preserved and retains its color and texture for 5-10 years. Popular for Indian corporate offices due to zero maintenance and natural aesthetic. Provides acoustic benefits, reducing noise by 8+ decibels.

Preserved Moss Zero Maintenance Acoustic Panels
Green Walls

Modular Green Wall System

Pre-planted panels or modules that interlock to create living walls. Each module contains its own growing media, plants, and irrigation connections. Benefits include easy installation, simple plant replacement, and scalability. Common systems in India include felt pocket systems, plastic tray systems, and soil-based panel systems.

Panel Systems Easy Installation Scalable
Plants

Money Plant (मनी प्लांट)

Common name in India for Epipremnum aureum (Pothos). Culturally significant in India as a symbol of prosperity and luck. One of the hardiest indoor plants, tolerating low light, irregular watering, and air conditioning. Removes formaldehyde, benzene, and xylene from air. Can be grown in water or soil.

Pothos Prosperity Air Purifying

N

Biophilic Design

Nature in the Space

One of three categories of biophilic design patterns identified by Terrapin Bright Green. Includes direct experiences of nature: visual connection with nature, non-visual connection (sounds, scents), non-rhythmic sensory stimuli, thermal and airflow variability, presence of water, dynamic and diffuse light, and connection with natural systems.

14 Patterns Direct Nature Terrapin
Biophilic Design

Natural Analogues

The second category of biophilic design patterns. Includes indirect evocations of nature: biomorphic forms and patterns, material connection with nature, and complexity and order (fractals). Natural analogues provide biophilic benefits even when living nature isn't feasible.

Biomorphic Forms Natural Materials Fractals
Technical

NASA Clean Air Study

A landmark 1989 NASA research project identifying indoor plants that effectively remove common air pollutants (benzene, formaldehyde, trichloroethylene). Top performers included Peace Lily, Snake Plant, English Ivy, Chrysanthemum, and Dracaena species. Foundational research for air-purifying plant recommendations worldwide.

Air Purification VOC Removal Research

AMC Plant Leasing Corporate Services
Wellness

Occupant Well-being

The physical, mental, and emotional health of building occupants. Biophilic design directly improves occupant well-being through stress reduction, improved air quality, visual comfort, and psychological restoration. Key metric for WELL Building Standard certification and corporate wellness programs.

Employee Health WELL Standard Workplace Wellness

P

Plants

Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum)

A top-performing air purifying plant that removes formaldehyde, benzene, trichloroethylene, xylene, and ammonia. One of few flowering plants suitable for low-light offices. NASA's Clean Air Study ranked it among the best air purifiers. Thrives in Indian AC environments but needs consistent moisture.

Air Purifying Flowering Low Light
Technical

Phytoremediation

The use of plants and their associated microorganisms to remove, degrade, or contain pollutants from air, water, or soil. Indoor plants perform air phytoremediation, absorbing pollutants through leaves and breaking them down in the root zone. Key mechanism behind air-purifying plant benefits.

Bioremediation Pollution Control Plant Science
Plants

Plantscaping

The professional design, installation, and maintenance of plants in interior and exterior environments. Combines horticultural expertise with design principles. Indian corporate plantscaping typically includes species selection, container design, placement planning, irrigation setup, and ongoing maintenance programs.

Interior Landscaping Corporate Greening Professional Services
Biophilic Design

Prospect and Refuge

A biophilic design pattern based on evolutionary preferences for spaces with both open views (prospect) and protective enclosure (refuge). Explains why corner offices, window seats, and booth seating feel comfortable. Design applications include varied ceiling heights, strategic partitions, and window seats with canopies.

Spatial Design Comfort Safety Psychology

R

Plants

Rubber Plant (Ficus elastica)

A popular statement plant for Indian offices with large, glossy leaves that effectively remove formaldehyde from air. Tolerates low light and infrequent watering once established. Available in green, burgundy, and variegated varieties. Can grow 6-10 feet indoors, creating strong visual impact.

Air Purifying Statement Plant Low Maintenance
Wellness

Restorative Environment

A space designed to promote psychological recovery from stress and mental fatigue. Biophilic design creates restorative environments through nature exposure, which activates the parasympathetic nervous system and reduces cortisol levels. Key elements include plants, natural views, water features, and natural materials.

Stress Reduction Mental Health Recovery Spaces

S

Plants

Snake Plant (Sansevieria)

Also called Mother-in-Law's Tongue or Sansevieria trifasciata. One of the most recommended office plants in India due to extreme hardiness—tolerates low light, irregular watering, and AC environments. Releases oxygen at night (unlike most plants), making it ideal for bedrooms and 24/7 work spaces. Removes formaldehyde, benzene, and nitrogen oxides.

Sansevieria Air Purifying Indestructible
Wellness

Sick Building Syndrome (SBS)

A condition where building occupants experience acute health symptoms (headaches, eye irritation, fatigue, respiratory issues) linked to time spent in a building. Caused by poor ventilation, VOC emissions, and inadequate humidity. Plants help mitigate SBS by purifying air, regulating humidity, and providing psychological benefits.

Indoor Air Quality Building Health VOCs
Technical

Stabilized Plants / Preserved Plants

Real plants treated with a glycerin-based solution that replaces natural sap, maintaining appearance without water, light, or maintenance. Used for moss walls, preserved flower arrangements, and stabilized foliage walls. Lifespan of 5-10 years. Popular in Indian offices where maintenance is challenging or where living plants won't survive.

Moss Art Zero Maintenance Preserved Greenery

T

Biophilic Design

Terrapin Bright Green

A sustainability consulting firm that published "14 Patterns of Biophilic Design" (2014), the most widely referenced framework for implementing biophilic design. The 14 patterns are grouped into Nature in the Space, Natural Analogues, and Nature of the Space categories. Essential reading for architects and designers.

14 Patterns Design Framework Research
Technical

Transpiration

The process by which plants release water vapor through their leaves. Indoor plants increase ambient humidity through transpiration, counteracting dry air from air conditioning. A single Areca Palm can transpire approximately 1 liter of water daily, naturally humidifying office environments.

Humidity Natural Cooling Evapotranspiration

U

Sustainability

Urban Heat Island Effect

The phenomenon where urban areas experience higher temperatures than surrounding rural areas due to heat absorption by concrete, asphalt, and buildings. Green roofs, green facades, and urban plantscaping help mitigate this effect—particularly critical in Indian metros like Delhi, Mumbai, and Chennai during summer.

Green Roofs Urban Greening Climate Mitigation

V

Green Walls

Vertical Garden

A garden that grows vertically using wall space rather than horizontal ground area. Includes living walls (plants in wall-mounted systems) and green facades (climbing plants on structures). Ideal for space-constrained Indian offices. Can be indoor or outdoor, simple DIY or complex commercial systems.

Living Wall Space-Saving Urban Gardening
Technical

VOCs (Volatile Organic Compounds)

Harmful chemicals that easily evaporate at room temperature, released from building materials, furniture, paints, cleaning products, and office equipment. Common VOCs include formaldehyde, benzene, xylene, and toluene. Cause headaches, respiratory issues, and long-term health problems. Plants can absorb and break down many VOCs.

Air Quality Formaldehyde Air Purifying Plants
Biophilic Design

Visual Connection with Nature

The first and most impactful of the 14 biophilic design patterns. A view of living natural elements, systems, and processes. Can include potted plants, green walls, nature views through windows, water features, or nature photography. Research shows even 5-minute exposure reduces stress and blood pressure.

14 Patterns Nature Views Stress Reduction

W

Wellness

WELL Building Standard

A building certification focused exclusively on human health and wellness, administered by the International WELL Building Institute (IWBI). Includes a "Biophilia - Qualitative" feature requiring biophilic design elements. Growing in adoption among Indian IT parks and Grade A office buildings.

Building Certification Human Health Corporate Wellness
Wellness

Workplace Wellness

Organizational programs and environmental designs that support employee physical and mental health. In India, leading companies invest ₹5,000-15,000 per employee annually in wellness initiatives. Biophilic design is increasingly recognized as a core component, with documented ROI of 3-5x through reduced absenteeism and increased productivity.

Employee Health Corporate Programs Productivity ROI
Biophilic Design

Wilson, Edward O.

Harvard biologist who popularized the term "biophilia" in his 1984 book of the same name. Wilson proposed that humans have an innate, evolutionary need to connect with nature and other living organisms. His work laid the scientific foundation for biophilic design.

Biophilia Harvard Evolutionary Biology

Z

Plants

ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

An extremely hardy plant native to East Africa, nicknamed "the plant you can't kill." Tolerates extreme low light (even fluorescent-only offices), irregular watering, dry air, and neglect. Has attractive glossy leaves and removes xylene and toluene from air. Ideal for Indian offices with minimal maintenance resources. New dark "Raven" variety adds dramatic visual interest.

Low Light Drought Tolerant Low Maintenance
