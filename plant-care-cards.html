What Are Plant Care Cards?

Plant care cards are printable quick-reference guides containing essential information for each plant: watering frequency (adjusted for Indian seasons), light requirements, humidity preferences, common problems and solutions, and quick care tips. Print them and attach to plant pots, keep at your desk, or use as training materials. Each card is designed for easy scanning during plant care routines.

How to Use Plant Care Cards

Get the most out of your downloaded care guides

1

Download & Print

Download individual cards or the complete bundle. Print on cardstock for durability, or regular paper for quick reference.

2

Attach to Plants

Use a plant stake or clip to attach cards directly to pots. This helps anyone caring for plants quickly find the right information.

3

Keep at Desk

Keep a set at your desk or in the office pantry for easy reference during watering rounds or plant care checks.

4

Train Your Team

Use cards for training housekeeping staff or colleagues on proper plant care. Visual guides make learning easy.
