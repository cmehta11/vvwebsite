Custom Design Service

Bespoke planters tailored to your architectural vision

Why Choose Custom Manufacturing?

  • Any Shape, Any Size: From organic sculptural forms to precise geometric designs, our in-house FRP/FRC facility can manufacture virtually any shape.
  • Brand Integration: Incorporate logos, color schemes, and design elements that align with your brand identity and architectural concept.
  • Material Flexibility: Choose from FRP, FRC, metal fabrication, or hybrid materials to meet specific weight, durability, or aesthetic requirements.
  • Quantity No Barrier: Whether you need 10 signature pieces or 1,000 matching planters for a campus-wide rollout, we scale seamlessly.

Our Custom Design Process

1
Consultation

Share your vision, sketches, or architectural drawings with our design team.

2
3D Modeling

We create detailed 3D renderings and technical specifications for your approval.

3
Prototype

Full-scale prototype manufactured for physical review and approval.

4
Production

Mass production at our facility with stringent quality control checks.

5
Delivery

Safe packaging and delivery to your site(s) across India.
