Who We Are

India's Leading Workplace Biophilia & Landscape Solutions Company

Vertvista Eco Solutions Pvt. Ltd. creates healthier, more vibrant, and productive environments for corporates, architects, developers, institutions, and facility managers.

Pan-India presence with operations across major cities.

We believe people thrive when surrounded by nature, and our work integrates greenery with design and functionality to elevate both aesthetics and well-being. Through our end-to-end solutions spanning indoor plants, maintenance, green walls, moss art, planters, landscape design, biophilic workspaces, and AMC services, we're transforming how the growing urban India connects with nature.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted biophilia and landscaping partner, bringing greenery and design into 10,000+ spaces.

Our Mission

To simplify and scale biophilic solutions by combining design, reliable operations, and emerging technology—making cities healthier and more inspiring.
