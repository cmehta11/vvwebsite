Quick Care Guide

Essential tips for keeping your office plants healthy

Watering

Check soil moisture before watering. Most office plants prefer to dry out slightly between waterings. Overwatering is the #1 killer!

Lighting

Most plants listed here tolerate low office light. Rotate plants quarterly for even growth. Avoid placing directly under AC vents.

Temperature

Office temperatures (20-26°C) are perfect for most tropical plants. Avoid sudden temperature changes and cold drafts.

Maintenance

Dust leaves monthly for better photosynthesis. Remove yellow leaves promptly. Consider our AMC for hassle-free care!
