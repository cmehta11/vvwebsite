## Technical Implementation

### Schema.org Types Used

1. **Organization** (Homepage)
   - Full company details
   - Service catalog
   - Geographic coverage
   - Expertise keywords

2. **Service** (All service pages)
   - Service type and name
   - Comprehensive descriptions
   - Provider details
   - Offer catalogs
   - Additional properties
   - knowsAbout fields

3. **Product** (Product pages)
   - Material specifications
   - Category classifications
   - Size variations
   - Customization options

4. **BreadcrumbList** (All pages)
   - Navigation context
   - Page hierarchy
   - URL structure

5. **LocalBusiness** (Company pages)
   - Contact information
   - Operating areas
   - Business hours (where applicable)

### Meta Tag Optimization

**Pattern Used**: Natural Language Question-Based
- Starts with conversational phrases: "Need...", "Looking for..."
- Includes specific locations (cities)
- Mentions concrete pricing/metrics
- Adds social proof (client counts, years)
- Stays within 150-160 characters

**Examples**:
```
"Need indoor plants for your office in Mumbai, Bangalore, or other Indian cities? Vertvista offers natural and artificial office plants with flexible rental (₹8,500/month+) or purchase options..."
```
