Questions

Frequently Asked

What is the best material for outdoor planters?

FRP (Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic) is ideal for outdoor use in India due to its weather resistance, lightweight nature, and durability. It withstands monsoon rains, UV exposure, and temperature variations. For premium outdoor installations, Corten steel provides an elegant rust patina that develops character over time.

What is the lead time for custom planters?

Custom FRP planters typically require 2-4 weeks from design approval to delivery. This includes mold creation, production, finishing, and quality control. Rush orders can be accommodated for an additional fee. Standard catalog items are available for immediate dispatch.

Can you match specific RAL colors?

Yes, our FRP planters can be finished in any RAL color. We also offer texture options including matte, gloss, and stone-effect finishes. Color matching is included in our custom design service with no additional charge for standard RAL colors.

Do planters come with drainage?

All our planters are designed with proper drainage in mind. FRP and metal planters include pre-drilled drainage holes. For indoor use, we provide matching saucers or can install internal drainage systems with reservoirs. Ceramic planters may require additional drainage solutions.

What is the minimum order quantity?

For custom designs requiring new molds, the minimum order is 5-10 pieces depending on size and complexity. Standard catalog items have no minimum order. For large projects, we offer volume discounts starting at 20+ pieces.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, we offer end-to-end services including delivery, placement, planting, and ongoing maintenance. Our team coordinates with architects and contractors for seamless project integration. Installation is available across all 6 cities we operate in.
