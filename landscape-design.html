Landscape Design & Build

Comprehensive Landscape Architecture | From Vision to Execution with In-House Experts

Transform outdoor environments into functional, sustainable spaces that enhance corporate image, employee wellbeing, and environmental performance.

At Vertvista, we offer complete landscape design and build services led by our in-house team of landscape architects, horticulturists, and project managers. From initial concept to final execution, we handle every aspect of landscape development—site planning, drainage engineering, irrigation systems, plant selection, hardscape design, lighting, and ongoing maintenance.

Whether you're developing a corporate campus, commercial property, residential complex, or institutional facility, our comprehensive approach delivers multi-dimensional benefits that generic landscaping solutions cannot match. We create spaces that optimize resources, reduce long-term maintenance costs, and provide lasting value.

Our Design-Build Process

Seamless journey from concept to completion

Site Analysis & Client Consultation

Comprehensive site assessment including topography, soil testing, drainage patterns, sun exposure, and existing conditions. We discuss your vision, functional requirements, budget, and timeline expectations.

1
2

Concept Development & Visualization

Our architects create preliminary design concepts with 3D renderings showing how the final landscape will look. We present multiple options with plant palettes, hardscape materials, and spatial layouts.

Design Refinement & Approvals

Based on your feedback, we refine the design and develop detailed construction drawings including planting plans, irrigation layouts, drainage schemes, and lighting designs. All necessary approvals obtained.

4

Procurement & Scheduling

We procure all materials, plants, and equipment while coordinating installation schedules to minimize disruption. Dedicated project manager keeps you informed throughout.

Professional Installation

Our experienced installation crews execute the design with precision. From soil preparation and hardscape construction to plant installation and system testing—everything handled professionally.

Establishment & Maintenance
