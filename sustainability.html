Our Sustainable Practices

Six pillars of environmental responsibility

Responsible Sourcing

We partner with certified nurseries and ethical suppliers to ensure sustainable plant cultivation.

  • No endangered species
  • Locally sourced when possible
  • Organic growing methods preferred
  • Fair trade moss from European partners

Water Conservation

Advanced irrigation systems and water management reduce consumption by up to 40%.

  • Drip irrigation systems with timers
  • Rainwater harvesting integration
  • Drought-tolerant plant selection
  • Soil moisture sensors

Eco-Friendly Materials

We prioritize sustainable, recyclable, and non-toxic materials in all our products.

  • Recyclable FRP planters
  • FSC-certified wooden elements
  • Non-toxic, water-based finishes
  • Biodegradable packaging

Waste Reduction & Recycling

Comprehensive waste management with 85% recycling rate across operations.

  • Composting plant trimmings
  • Recycling planter materials
  • Minimal packaging design
  • Zero single-use plastics

Energy Efficiency

Our operations and solutions contribute to building energy savings.

  • LED grow lights for indoor plants
  • Solar-powered irrigation timers
  • Green walls reduce HVAC loads by 30%
  • Electric vehicle fleet (pilot program)

Biodiversity Support

Promoting urban biodiversity through native plant species and pollinator-friendly designs.

  • Native species prioritization
  • Pollinator-friendly plant selection
  • Bird-friendly façade designs
  • Urban wildlife habitat creation

Contributing to Green Building Standards

Our solutions help achieve LEED, IGBC, and WELL Building certifications

LEED Points Contribution

Our biophilic installations contribute to multiple LEED credit categories:

  • • Sustainable Sites (SS)
  • • Water Efficiency (WE)
  • • Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ)
  • • Innovation in Design (ID)

IGBC Green Building Ratings

Supporting Indian Green Building Council standards through:

  • • Native landscaping credits
  • • Water conservation measures
  • • Urban heat island mitigation
  • • Indoor air quality improvement

WELL Building Standard

Enhancing occupant health and wellness:

  • • Biophilic design features
  • • Air quality optimization
  • • Circadian lighting support
  • • Stress reduction environments
