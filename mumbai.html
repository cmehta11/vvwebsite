Our Mumbai Services

Tailored biophilia solutions for Bangalore's unique climate and corporate culture

Tech Office Plant Solutions

Specialized plant selection for air-conditioned tech offices, startup spaces, and open-plan layouts common in Bangalore's IT sector. Low-maintenance varieties perfect for 24/7 office environments.

Campus Landscaping

Comprehensive landscape design for large IT campuses and corporate headquarters. Experience with Infosys, Wipro-style campus greening including outdoor spaces, walkways, and recreational areas.

Vertical Gardens for Tech Parks

Green wall installations designed for Bangalore's moderate climate. Perfect for tech park atriums, lobby areas, and exterior facades with automated irrigation systems.

Maintenance AMC

Dedicated 15-person Bangalore team providing weekly/bi-weekly maintenance. We understand Bangalore's climate challenges and plant needs throughout the year.

Biophilic Design for Startups

Budget-friendly biophilic solutions for growing startups. Help create investor-ready, employee-friendly office spaces that boost productivity and retention.

Preserved Moss Art

Zero-maintenance moss walls perfect for Bangalore's startup culture. European preserved moss in custom designs, logos, and accent walls requiring no watering or sunlight.
