## 🎯 Two-Front Strategy: Traditional SEO + LLM Optimization

### Understanding the Difference

| Aspect | Traditional SEO | LLM/AI Optimization |
|--------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Goal** | Rank on Google/Bing | Get recommended by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity |
| **Key Factors** | Keywords, backlinks, page speed | Structured data, comprehensive content, factual accuracy |
| **Content Style** | Keyword-optimized | Natural language, Q&A format, entity-rich |
| **Technical** | Meta tags, sitemaps | Schema.org, clear hierarchies, machine-readable |
